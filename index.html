Operator: Changzhou Yuzhenxuan Trading Co., Ltd. (Changzhou, Jiangsu, China)

Brands: YUZHENXUAN (https://yzxmetals.com) and GAOZHEN (https://gaozhenmetal.com)

Last updated: November 2026

1. What this application is

YZX Media is an internal publishing tool used by Changzhou Yuzhenxuan Trading Co., Ltd. (the Company, we) to upload and manage video content on the YouTube channels the Company operates for its own brands, YUZHENXUAN and GAOZHEN.

It is used by the Company's own publishing staff. It is not distributed or made available to the public, and it is not a service for third parties. People who watch our videos do not interact with it. All content published through it is produced by the Company.

2. What Google data the application accesses, and why

The application requests two YouTube Data API v3 scopes, and no others.

youtube.upload — uploading a video file to the Company's own YouTube channel, setting its title, description, tags and visibility at the time of upload, and setting a custom thumbnail for it.

youtube.readonly — reading channel metadata and the publish status of videos the Company has uploaded, so staff can verify a scheduled upload completed.

Access is limited to the YouTube channel or channels the Company operates for its own brands: [YOUTUBE_CHANNEL_URL]. The application is not authorized against, and does not read, any other channel or any other Google account.

In practice the application reads only that channel's title, channel ID and video count, plus metadata and publish status for videos the Company itself has uploaded. It does not read viewer data: not view counts, watch history, subscriber lists, comments, replies, ratings or private messages.

3. How we store and protect it

OAuth credentials. After an operator authorizes the application, the refresh token issued by Google is stored only on the Company-managed computers where the publishing scripts run, in a local configuration file that is not committed to source control and is not transmitted over the network. It is used only to request short-lived access tokens.

Access tokens. An access token is valid for approximately one hour, is held in memory during a publish operation, and is discarded when that operation ends. A new token is requested when one is needed rather than storing tokens long-term.

Content published. Videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags submitted through the application are published to the Company's own YouTube channel and are visible there. Draft metadata is held only in local working files until submitted.

Review of Google data. The metadata read through youtube.readonly is shown to publishing staff for verification. It is not copied into external systems and is not used for profiling or advertising.

4. What we do not do with Google user data

The application does not sell, rent, trade or otherwise provide Google user data to third parties. It is not used for advertising or to build advertising audiences. The application does not post comments or replies, does not send direct messages, and does not subscribe or unsubscribe any account; it does not automate any interaction between our channels and the people who watch them. It does not read, store or export personal information about viewers.

Artificial intelligence. Video material published through the application — footage, written copy and music — is produced with the assistance of internal AI tools. The imagery is a schematic visualization of product and process, not footage of the Company's own plant, and the Company's site and video material carry the same notice. Those AI tools create video assets only: they are not connected to Google account credentials, are not given access to the YouTube API, and do not read, process or store any Google user data. API calls carrying OAuth credentials are made only by the local publishing scripts on the Company's computers.

5. Your rights and how to revoke access

Because the application does not read or store data about people who watch our videos, there is no viewer data held by us that can be exported or deleted.

If you are a member of staff who has authorized the application, you can withdraw that authorization at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, where the entry for YZX Media can be removed. Revoking access stops the application from obtaining new access tokens, and the refresh token held locally stops working.

Information about the Company's own channel and its videos is managed through the YouTube channel backend. Requests relating to that content can be sent to the contact address below.
